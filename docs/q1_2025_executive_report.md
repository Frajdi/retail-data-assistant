### 1. Key Operational & Financial Anomalies

This points to potential localized payment gateway friction, unexpected customs duties, or extended shipping timelines.
2. **Category Margin Compression**:
   * While the company achieved an overall **52.16% gross profit margin**, margin performance varied substantially across product lines. **Women’s Suits** (**39.32% margin**) and **Men’s Socks** (**39.83% margin**) suffered significant cost-of-goods pressure, lagging far behind high-margin categories like **Men's Suits & Sport Coats** (**59.75% margin**) and **Men's Outerwear** (**56.23% margin**).
3. **Severe Acquisition Channel Imbalance**:
   * Direct **Search advertising** accounts for **70.56%** ($299,166.15) of total net revenue, whereas high-margin lifecycle channels like **Email** contributed only **4.90%** ($20,783.54) and **Display** contributed **3.80%** ($16,133.58). 
4. **Low In-Quarter Repeat Purchase Frequency**:
   * **95.87%** of active Q1 buyers (5,884 out of 6,137) made only a single purchase during the quarter. Repeat buyers (253 users) generated **$43,736.91**, indicating that revenue scaling is almost entirely reliant on new customer acquisition rather than retention.
